## SECTION C - STATEMENT OF WORK

### C.1 Background

ORM administers the Regional Mobility Data Program (RMDP), which collects
ridership, on-time performance, and fare equity data from 47 metropolitan
transit authorities. ORM requires analytical and policy support to convert this
data into decision-ready products for Congressional reporting and grant
oversight.

### C.2 Scope

The Contractor shall provide analytical, data quality, and policy support
services across four task areas.

### C.3 Task Area 1 - Data Quality and Validation

**C.3.1** The Contractor shall perform quarterly data quality reviews of all 47
transit authority submissions.

**C.3.2** The Contractor shall document every validation exception in the ORM
Data Exception Register within five (5) business days of discovery.

**C.3.3** The Contractor must maintain a validation error rate below two percent
(2%) across all reviewed submissions.

### C.4 Task Area 2 - Analytical Products

**C.4.1** The Contractor shall produce twelve (12) monthly Regional Mobility
Snapshots per period of performance.

**C.4.2** The Contractor shall produce four (4) quarterly Trend Analyses
incorporating fare equity and on-time performance indicators.

**C.4.3** All analytical products shall be delivered in both PDF and machine
readable CSV formats.

**C.4.4** The Contractor is required to reproduce any published figure on
request within three (3) business days, including the underlying query.

### C.5 Task Area 3 - Policy Support

**C.5.1** The Contractor shall prepare Congressional response packages within
ten (10) business days of tasking.

**C.5.2** The Contractor shall provide subject matter expertise at up to
twenty-four (24) stakeholder meetings per period of performance.

**C.5.3** Policy recommendations must be traceable to specific data findings and
must cite the source dataset and collection period.

### C.6 Task Area 4 - Program Management

**C.6.1** The Contractor shall deliver a Monthly Status Report by the tenth
(10th) calendar day of each month.

**C.6.2** The Contractor shall conduct a Post-Award Kickoff Meeting within ten
(10) calendar days of contract award.

**C.6.3** The Contractor shall maintain a Quality Assurance Surveillance Plan
(QASP) aligned to the performance standards in Attachment B.

**C.6.4** The Contractor will designate a single Program Manager who serves as
the primary point of contact for all contractual matters.

### C.7 Key Personnel

**C.7.1** The following positions are designated Key Personnel: Program Manager,
Lead Data Scientist, and Senior Policy Analyst.

**C.7.2** The Contractor shall not substitute Key Personnel without written
approval from the Contracting Officer.

**C.7.3** Key Personnel resumes are required and shall not exceed two (2) pages
each.

## SECTION M - EVALUATION FACTORS FOR AWARD

### M.1 Basis for Award

Award will be made to the offeror whose proposal represents the best value to
the Government, using a trade-off process. Technical merit is significantly more
important than price.

### M.2 Evaluation Factors, in Descending Order of Importance

**M.2.1 Factor 1 - Technical Approach.** The Government will evaluate the degree
to which the offeror demonstrates a sound, feasible approach to each of the four
task areas. To receive a rating of Outstanding, the offeror must demonstrate all
of the following for **each** of the four task areas:

- a described methodology, including tools and data handling steps;
- a worked example drawn from prior performance;
- a quantified quality control mechanism with a stated threshold;
- an identified risk with a specific mitigation; and
- staffing by labor category with hours allocated.

**M.2.2 Factor 2 - Management Approach.** The Government will evaluate the
staffing plan, quality control approach, risk management, and communication
cadence.

**M.2.3 Factor 3 - Key Personnel.** The Government will evaluate the
qualifications of the three Key Personnel against the requirements in C.7.

**M.2.4 Factor 4 - Past Performance.** The Government will evaluate the relevance
and quality of the submitted references.

**M.2.5 Factor 5 - Price.** Price will be evaluated for reasonableness and
completeness.
