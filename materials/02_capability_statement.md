## Core Competencies

**Transportation data quality engineering.** MAG builds and operates validation
pipelines for large multi-jurisdiction transit datasets. Our validation
framework, MERIDIAN-QC, applies rule-based and statistical outlier checks and
produces an auditable exception record for every flagged value.

**Analytical product development.** We convert operational data into recurring
decision products - dashboards, snapshots, and trend reports - designed around
what the reader must decide, not around what the data happens to contain.

**Transportation policy analysis.** Our policy team supports Federal and state
clients with Congressional response packages, regulatory comment analysis, and
grant program evaluation.

**Reproducible analysis.** Every published MAG figure is backed by a versioned
query stored in our analysis repository, so any number can be regenerated on
demand.

## Differentiators

1. **Reproducibility by default.** Every figure MAG publishes carries a query
   identifier. In our five years on the FTA National Transit Database Support
   contract, we met every reproduction request within two business days.
2. **Transit domain depth.** Twenty-two of our analysts have prior direct
   experience working at or with a metropolitan transit authority.
3. **Low staff turnover.** Voluntary turnover across the analytical staff was
   6.4 percent in calendar year 2025, against an industry benchmark near 18
   percent.
