## Purpose

This amendment (1) extends the proposal due date, (2) revises Section L page and
format requirements, (3) revises one Section C performance requirement, (4) adds
a Section M evaluation subfactor, and (5) incorporates the Q&A responses issued
separately. Amendment 0001 was administrative only and extended the question
deadline; it made no substantive change.

Offerors shall acknowledge receipt of this amendment in their cover letter.

---

## CHANGE 1 - Proposal Due Date

**Section:** Cover page

- **WAS:** Proposals Due: 27 February 2026, 2:00 PM Eastern
- **IS NOW:** Proposals Due: **13 March 2026, 2:00 PM Eastern**

---

## CHANGE 2 - Volume I Page Limit

**Section:** L.3.1

- **WAS:** Volume I shall not exceed ten (10) pages, excluding Key Personnel
  resumes and the cover page.
- **IS NOW:** Volume I shall not exceed **fifteen (15) pages**, excluding Key
  Personnel resumes, the cover page, and the Section 508 Conformance
  Certification appendix.

*Rationale: in response to questions regarding the volume of content required by
Section M.*

---

## CHANGE 3 - Font Requirement

**Section:** L.6

- **WAS:** Body text shall be Times New Roman, 12 point, single spaced.
- **IS NOW:** Body text shall be **Times New Roman or Arial, 11 point minimum**,
  single spaced.

All other L.6 format requirements are unchanged.

---

## CHANGE 4 - Validation Error Rate Threshold

**Section:** C.3.3

- **WAS:** The Contractor must maintain a validation error rate below two percent
  (2%) across all reviewed submissions.
- **IS NOW:** The Contractor must maintain a validation error rate below **one
  and one-half percent (1.5%)** across all reviewed submissions, measured
  quarterly beginning with the second full quarter of performance.

**Attachment B is revised accordingly:** the Validation error rate threshold
changes from "Below 2%" to "Below 1.5%".

---

## CHANGE 5 - New Evaluation Subfactor

**Section:** M.2.2

The following subfactor is **added** to Factor 2, Management Approach:

> **M.2.2.1 Surge Capacity.** The Government will evaluate the offeror's
> demonstrated ability to absorb a 25 percent increase in submission volume
> within a single quarter without a deliverable slip. Offerors shall describe the
> specific mechanism, not merely assert capacity.

---

## CHANGE 6 - Number of Reporting Authorities

**Section:** C.1 and C.3.1

- **WAS:** 47 metropolitan transit authorities
- **IS NOW:** **49** metropolitan transit authorities

*Two authorities joined the Regional Mobility Data Program in January 2026.*

---

## Items Explicitly NOT Changed

For the avoidance of doubt, this amendment does **not** change:

- The five required Volume I sections or their order (L.3).
- The three past performance references requirement (L.4.1).
- The Key Personnel positions or the two-page resume limit (C.7).
- The Section 508 Conformance Certification requirement (Attachment C-1).
- The Facility Clearance requirement (Attachment C-3).
- The estimated base period value of $2.4 million.
