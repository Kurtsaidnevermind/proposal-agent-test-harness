# Questions and Answers

**Solicitation:** DOT-ORM-2026-R-0147
**Issued:** 03 February 2026 (incorporated by Amendment 0002)

> **FICTIONAL TRAINING DOCUMENT.** Fixed test material for the Proposal Agent
> Test Harness. Part of the long-document set used by test F4.

---

**Q1.** Section L.3.1 limits Volume I to ten pages, but Section M.2.1 requires
five distinct content elements for each of four task areas to earn an Outstanding
rating. Will the Government reconsider the page limit?

**A1.** Yes. See Amendment 0002, Change 2. The Volume I page limit is increased
to fifteen pages.

---

**Q2.** Is the Section 508 Conformance Certification counted against the Volume I
page limit?

**A2.** No. See Amendment 0002, Change 2. The certification appendix is excluded
from the page count.

---

**Q3.** May offerors propose Arial rather than Times New Roman?

**A3.** Yes. See Amendment 0002, Change 3.

---

**Q4.** Is a Facility Clearance required at the time of proposal submission?

**A4.** No. Attachment C-3 requires the Contractor to hold, or obtain within
thirty days of award, a Facility Clearance at the Confidential level. An offeror
without a current clearance is not ineligible, but shall describe its plan to
obtain one.

---

**Q5.** How many exceptions did the incumbent record in the most recent reporting
year?

**A5.** The Government does not release incumbent performance data during an
active procurement.

---

**Q6.** Section C.4.1 requires twelve monthly Snapshots per period of
performance. Does the base period, which begins mid-fiscal-year, require twelve?

**A6.** Yes. Twelve Snapshots are required in each twelve-month period of
performance regardless of fiscal year alignment.

---

**Q7.** Will the Government provide historical submission data for offerors to
demonstrate their methodology?

**A7.** No. Offerors shall demonstrate methodology using their own prior
performance data.

---

**Q8.** Are the three past performance references required to be Federal
contracts?

**A8.** No. References may be Federal, state, local, or commercial, provided they
are of similar scope and dollar value and were performed within the last five
years.

---

**Q9.** May a single individual be proposed for two Key Personnel positions?

**A9.** No. The three Key Personnel positions in C.7.1 shall be filled by three
distinct individuals.

---

**Q10.** Does the validation error rate threshold apply from contract start?

**A10.** See Amendment 0002, Change 4. The revised threshold of 1.5 percent is
measured quarterly beginning with the second full quarter of performance.
