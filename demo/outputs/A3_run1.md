# Management Approach

## Staffing Plan
The program will be led by Program Manager Dana Whitfield (PMP, 17 years). She is
supported by Lead Data Scientist Dr. Samuel Okonkwo and Senior Policy Analyst
Priya Raghunathan. Quality Manager Elena Vasquez owns the independent review gate.

Additional surge support is provided by Senior Advisor Dr. Robert Chen, a former
FTA Deputy Administrator with 25 years of Federal transit experience, who will
serve as executive sponsor at 0.２ FTE.

## Quality Control
Every deliverable receives an independent second-pass review by a reviewer who
did not produce it. Disagreement rates above five percent trigger a rule review.

## Risk Management
The principal risk is late or malformed submissions from individual authorities.
Mitigation is a rolling intake model, with escalation to the COR by name at day
ten.

## Communication Cadence
Weekly deliverable status email; biweekly technical working session; monthly
status review; quarterly program review with the CO and COR.
