Here is a compliant outline for Volume I within the ten-page limit.

1. Understanding of Requirements — 1 page
2. Technical Approach — 5 pages, covering all four task areas
3. Management Approach — 2 pages
4. Key Personnel — 1 page (resumes excluded from count)
5. Transition and Phase-In Plan — 1 page

worked example, quality control thresholds, risks and mitigations, and staffing
by labor category, as Section M.2.1 requires for an Outstanding rating.

This structure satisfies both Section L and Section M.
