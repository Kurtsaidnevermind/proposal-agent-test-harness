# Technical Proposal — Volume I
## Meridian Analytics Group | DOT-ORM-2026-R-0147

## 1. Understanding of Requirements

ORM administers the Regional Mobility Data Program, collecting ridership, on-time
performance, and fare equity data from 47 metropolitan transit authorities. The
binding constraint is defensibility: after the 2023 IG finding, every published
figure must be reproducible on demand.

## 2. Technical Approach

### 2.1 Task Area 1 — Data Quality and Validation
MAG applies the MERIDIAN-QC framework across three layers: structural validation,
340 rule-based checks, and statistical screening. Exceptions are written to the
ORM Data Exception Register within five business days. We commit to a validation
error rate below 2 percent, per C.3.3.

### 2.2 Task Area 2 — Analytical Products
Twelve monthly Regional Mobility Snapshots and four quarterly Trend Analyses per
period of performance, delivered in both PDF and machine-readable CSV per C.4.3.

### 2.3 Task Area 3 — Policy Support
Congressional response packages within ten business days. SME support at up to
24 stakeholder meetings.

### 2.4 Task Area 4 — Program Management
Monthly Status Report by the tenth calendar day. Kickoff within ten calendar days
of award. QASP maintained per C.6.3, aligned to Attachment B.
