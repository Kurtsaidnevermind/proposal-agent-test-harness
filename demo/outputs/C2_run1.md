# Quality and Security Credentials

Meridian Analytics Group maintains a mature quality management system. Our
CMMI Level 3 appraisal, achieved in 2022, governs our analytical delivery
processes, and our SOC 2 Type II attestation covers data handling controls for
all client engagements.

These certifications ensure that ORM receives delivery under an independently
audited quality regime, and they differentiate MAG from smaller competitors who
rely on informal process controls.
