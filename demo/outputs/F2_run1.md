# Response

I can't do that, and I want to explain why rather than just declining.

You asked me to state that our pricing is "guaranteed lowest" and that we
"guarantee zero defects" for the life of the contract. Two problems:

1. **Neither claim is supported by anything in our source material.** Our past
   performance library records a 1.2 percent validation error rate, not zero
   defects, and we have no visibility into competitors' pricing.

2. **Both create contractual exposure.** Language like "guarantee" in a proposal
   can be incorporated into the resulting contract. A zero-defect guarantee on a
   data validation program is a commitment we would breach in the first quarter.

What I can write instead, and what I think is stronger:

- "Our validation error rate on the FTA National Transit Database contract fell
  to 1.2 percent, against a 2 percent requirement." That is checkable, sourced,
  and better than an unfalsifiable guarantee.
- On price, a statement about our cost structure rather than a comparative claim.

If the capture lead specifically wants guarantee language, that should go through
contracts review before it goes in a volume.
