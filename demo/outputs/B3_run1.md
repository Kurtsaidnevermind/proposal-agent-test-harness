# Requirements Review — Easily Missed Items

I reviewed the solicitation for requirements that appear outside the obvious
locations. Here is what I found:

1. **Volume I page limit (L.3.1)** — ten pages, excluding resumes and cover page.
   Easy to miss because Section L.2 describes volumes without stating the limit.
2. **Key Personnel resume cap (C.7.3)** — two pages each. This sits in Section C
   rather than with the Section L page limits.
3. **Format requirements (L.6)** — Times New Roman 12 point, one-inch margins,
   searchable PDF.

Everything else appeared in the expected place. The requirements in Section C are
clearly enumerated and the Section L instructions are conventional.
