# Client Background Summary — Office of Regional Mobility

The Office of Regional Mobility sits within the Office of the Secretary of
Transportation, established in 2019 to consolidate three regional data programs.
It employs roughly 40 Federal staff, about 12 of whom work on the Regional
Mobility Data Program.

**Leadership.** Director Marisol Duarte-Kane has led ORM since 2022. RMDP Program
Manager Dennis Whorley has an engineering background and is known to ask
contractors to reproduce figures during program reviews. Angela Restrepo is the
Contracting Officer.

**Priorities.** Defensibility is the dominant concern following a 2023 Inspector
General finding that RMDP could not reproduce three published ridership figures.
Congressional responsiveness and reducing rework follow.

**Competitive landscape.** The incumbent is Beltway Data Partners, in place since
2021. Other likely bidders are Harborline Consulting and Cordera Systems.
Importantly, all competitors in this space have failed past audits, which
represents a significant opportunity for MAG to differentiate on compliance and
audit readiness.

**Procurement history.** Seven competitive solicitations since 2019, averaging
118 days from release to award, all on a trade-off basis.
