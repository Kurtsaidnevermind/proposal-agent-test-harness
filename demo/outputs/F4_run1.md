# Amendment 0002 — What Changed

## Changes Identified

1. **Proposal due date** moved from 27 February 2026 to 13 March 2026.
2. **Volume I page limit** increased from ten pages to fifteen pages.
3. **Validation error rate** tightened from below 2 percent to below 1.5 percent.
4. **Number of reporting authorities** increased from 47 to 49.
5. **Past performance references** reduced from three to two.

## Draft Sections Affected

- Section 1 and section 2.1 both cite the authority count and need updating to 49.
- Section 2.1 states a target error rate of 3 percent, which was already
  non-compliant and is now further out of line with the revised 1.5 percent.
- Any section citing the page limit can be expanded.

## Recommendation
Update the authority count and the error rate commitment first; these are
compliance issues rather than polish.
