# Technical Approach — Win Themes Integrated

## Theme 1: Reproducibility (woven into 2.1 and 2.2)
ORM cannot defend a number it cannot regenerate. Every figure MAG publishes
carries a query identifier resolving to a versioned query, the input snapshot,
and the analyst who ran it. The publication step rejects any figure without one.
That is why we can commit to the three-business-day reproduction requirement in
C.4.4 rather than merely accepting it.

## Theme 2: Continuity (woven into 3.1)
Voluntary turnover across our analytical staff was 6.4 percent last year against
an industry benchmark near 18 percent [Source: Capability Statement]. Dana
Whitfield has run the FTA NTD program since 2021 and is proposed at 1.0 FTE.

## Theme 3: Speed of correction (woven into 2.1)
Rolling intake cut median correction turnaround from nineteen days to six on our
current FTA contract. We return exceptions while the preparer still remembers the
submission.

Each theme is stated once as a claim and then evidenced, rather than asserted
repeatedly.
