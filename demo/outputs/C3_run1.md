# Numeric Consistency Review

I checked the draft for internal numeric consistency and found the following:

1. **Transit authority count.** Section 1 says 45 metropolitan transit
   authorities; section 2.1 says 47. The RFP (C.1) says 47, so section 1 is
   wrong.

2. **Staffing count.** Section 3.1 proposes a "14 FTE team" while section 5
   refers to "our team of 11 analysts." These cannot both be right.

Recommend reconciling both before submission. Otherwise the numbers in the draft
appear sound.
